## Top 5 (ship list)

### 1. No back button on greenway detail page -- impact 12.0
- **Flagged by:** Information Architect (severity: critical)
- **Cross-scores:** brand 5, mobile 10, a11y 9 (mean 8.0, spread 5)
- **Top reason:** "Absent persistent bottom nav or tab bar is the single biggest mobile usability failure" -- Mobile
- **Suggested fix:** Add a `<- Greenways` back-link anchored top-left in the hero, styled in white with low opacity so it sits over the brick without competing with the h1.
- **Where to look:** `apps/nextjs/src/app/greenways/[slug]/page.tsx`

### 2. No persistent navigation on any inner page -- impact 11.5
- **Flagged by:** Information Architect (severity: critical)
- **Cross-scores:** brand 4, mobile 10, a11y 9 (mean 7.7, spread 6 -- high disagreement)
- **Top reason:** "Absent persistent navigation destroys landmark structure and leaves assistive technology users with no consistent way to orient" -- A11y
- **Suggested fix:** Add a slim persistent header in RootLayout with the 'clt' wordmark (linking to '/') and four primary-nav links (Greenways, Deals, Parking, Map). On mobile, use a fixed bottom tab bar matching the Expo tab pattern.
- **Where to look:** `apps/nextjs/src/app/layout.tsx`

### 3. No back navigation on greenway detail (mobile) -- impact 11.5
- **Flagged by:** Mobile-First Designer (severity: critical)
- **Cross-scores:** ia 10, brand 4, a11y 9 (mean 7.7, spread 6)
- **Top reason:** "No back navigation on detail pages is a critical wayfinding failure" -- IA
- **Suggested fix:** Add a sticky back chevron in the top-left of the hero (absolute positioned, white, 44px tap target).
- **Where to look:** `apps/nextjs/src/app/greenways/[slug]/page.tsx`

### 4. No global cross-section navigation on mobile web -- impact 11.0
- **Flagged by:** Mobile-First Designer (severity: critical)
- **Cross-scores:** ia 10, brand 3, a11y 9 (mean 7.3, spread 7 -- highest disagreement)
- **Top reason:** "No global cross-section navigation is a fundamental IA failure" -- IA
- **Suggested fix:** Add a fixed bottom nav bar with four icon+label tabs using `fixed bottom-0 inset-x-0 pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]`.
- **Where to look:** `apps/nextjs/src/app/layout.tsx`

### 5. VERIFIED badge: green text on brick is near-invisible (~1.2:1) -- impact 9.5
- **Flagged by:** Accessibility Auditor (severity: critical)
- **Cross-scores:** ia 3, brand 9, mobile 7 (mean 6.3, spread 6)
- **Top reason:** "The VERIFIED badge's green-on-brick ~1.2:1 contrast is an active color system failure" -- Brand
- **Suggested fix:** Override badge rendering on brick backgrounds: use white text with a semi-transparent dark pill, or move the badge below the hero into the cream section.
- **Where to look:** `apps/nextjs/src/components/LastVerifiedBadge.tsx`

## Dissent watch (panel disagreed)

### 1. No global cross-section navigation on mobile web (mobile-005) -- spread 7
- IA scored 10 (fundamental structure failure), Brand scored 3 (fix is architecture, not visual expression), A11y scored 9 (landmark structure destroyed). This is a taste call: Brand correctly notes the fix is nav chrome, not visual design, but the functional impact is the highest in the review.

### 2. No persistent navigation on any inner page (ia-002) -- spread 6
- Mobile scored 10, A11y scored 9, Brand scored 4. Brand's lower score reflects that navigation architecture is outside visual brand scope, but the functional consensus is that this is the #1 structural gap.

### 3. No back navigation on greenway detail (mobile-002) -- spread 6
- IA scored 10, A11y scored 9, Brand scored 4. Same pattern: navigation is structural, not brand -- but universally agreed as blocking.

## Persona-only signal

- **Brand & Visual Director's solo pick:** brand-002 -- Contribute page background is flat olive-gray instead of cream. Mean cross-score 2.3. Other personas saw it as a consistency bug; Brand sees it as a product-identity rupture. Worth fixing because the cream system is load-bearing for the Queen City craft feel.
- **Accessibility Auditor's solo pick:** a11y-002 -- No skip-to-content link anywhere in the app. Mean cross-score 3.0. Other personas see this as a minor accessibility improvement; for keyboard and switch-access users on the 63-trail list, it's a genuine dead-end. WCAG 2.4.1 Level A requirement.
